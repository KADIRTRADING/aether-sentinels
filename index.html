Select Map

How to Play

Goal: Stop the Aether swarm from reaching your Core. Each leak costs a life.

Build: Tap a tower in the tray, then tap a build node (⬡) on the map.

Upgrade: Tap a placed tower to upgrade its path or sell it.

Strategy: Each tower has a unique role — combine slows, splash, chain-lightning, and buffs. Bosses resist single strategies, so diversify.

Heroes: Deploy soldier heroes anywhere on the field. Drag one hero onto another of the same weapon to fuse them into a stronger weapon: Pistol → SMG → Rifle → Sniper → Minigun → Rocket Launcher.

Waves: Press Start Wave to summon. Survive all waves — including the Boss — to win the map.

Settings

Tip: place Aegis Pylons ◈ near clusters of towers, and set your Railguns to Strongest target mode to focus bosses.
