AETHERSENTINELS

Strategic Tower Defense

v1.3.0

Select Map

How to Play

Goal: Stop the Aether swarm from reaching your Core. Each leak costs a life.

Build: Tap a tower card in the tray, then tap a glowing build node (⬡) beside the path.

Upgrade: Tap a placed tower to upgrade its tier, change its targeting priority, or sell it.

Two currencies

⬢ Gold — earned during a battle from kills and cleared waves. Spent on building and upgrading towers and heroes. It resets every match.

🪙 Coins — persist between matches. Spent to permanently level up a unit (stronger damage/health). Earned by clearing maps or watching a rewarded ad.

Units

Towers are fixed to build nodes. Each has a role — chain lightning (half-armor energy), area slow, splash, armor-piercing sniper, support buffs, and armor-ignoring poison. Combine roles: bosses resist single strategies.

Heroes 🪖 deploy anywhere and can be dragged to reposition. Drag one hero onto another of the same weapon to fuse them: Pistol → SMG → Rifle → Sniper → Minigun → Rocket Launcher.

Abilities: ☄ Orbital Strike (tap to arm, tap the map to fire) and ❄ Cryo Pulse (freezes everything) — both on cooldown.

Waves: Press Start Wave to summon. Build between waves. Survive all waves — including the Boss — to win.

Controls: mouse or touch. Press M to mute, Space to start a wave, P to pause, 1/2/3 for speed.

Settings

Reduced motion limits screen shake and particle effects. Tip: place Aegis Pylons ◈ near clusters of towers, and set Railguns to Strongest to focus bosses.
